1. What JT Books is

JT Books is software that JT operates to deliver bookkeeping services. With your permission, it reads your QuickBooks Online accounting data through Intuit's official connection and uses it to prepare month-end review lists, client questions and monthly financial reports. JT runs the software on its own systems. You do not install anything.

3. Read-only access

JT Books does not create, edit or delete transactions, accounts, invoices or other records in QuickBooks Online, and it cannot move money. Any change to your books is made separately, by people, as described in your engagement letter.
