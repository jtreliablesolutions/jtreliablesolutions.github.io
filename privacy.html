Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Last updated October 1, 2026

JT Reliable Solutions, LLC ("JT", "we", "us") is a Georgia company based in Atlanta that provides bookkeeping, business consulting, and healthcare and logistics analytics services. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, how we protect it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

In short: we use your information only to deliver the services you hire us for. We do not sell it, rent it, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. Our QuickBooks connection is read-only: we can see your books but cannot change them. You can disconnect at any time.

1. What this policy covers

  • This website and email, phone or form contact with us.
  • Our client services (bookkeeping, consulting and analytics engagements).
  • JT Books, our software that connects to QuickBooks Online on a client's behalf to prepare month-end review lists and reports.
  • JT Healthcare Analytics, our analytics web pages, which read files a user selects inside the user's own browser.

Each client engagement also has a signed engagement letter. Where a healthcare client shares protected health information, a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) applies as well. If either document conflicts with this policy, that document controls for that client.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

Your name, business name, email address, phone number, mailing address and anything else you choose to send us when you contact us, sign an engagement letter, or pay an invoice. We do not store payment card numbers. Payments go through our payment processor.

Client business records

To do the work, clients give us financial records such as bank and card statements, receipts, invoices, payroll summaries and prior-year reports. Analytics clients may give us billing, scheduling or discharge exports, or order, inventory, shipment and maintenance records.

QuickBooks Online data (through JT Books)

When an authorized user of your QuickBooks Online company approves the connection on Intuit's sign-in page, Intuit gives JT Books read-only access to your company's accounting data under Intuit's "Accounting" permission. JT Books reads only what the monthly close needs:

Data readWhy we read it
Company ID and company nameTo link the connection to the right client
Chart of accounts and account balancesCategorization checks, cash position, negative balance alerts
Transactions (date, type, amount, account, payee or customer name, memo)The month-end review list: uncategorized items, possible duplicates, unusual amounts
Open invoices (customer name, amount, due date, balance)Money-owed tracking and invoices more than 60 days late
Attachment records (whether a receipt is attached, file name)Flags for missing receipts
Profit and loss reportThe client's monthly report and trend chart

JT Books does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks. It does not read payroll, employee Social Security numbers or bank login credentials, and it never sees your Intuit password. Intuit handles the sign-in.

Healthcare analytics files

The JT Healthcare Analytics pages read the CSV file you select inside your own web browser. The file is not uploaded to us or anyone else, and nothing from it is stored after you close the page. When a healthcare client sends us data for an engagement, we receive it only under a signed BAA, through a secure channel, and we limit it to the minimum needed. We prefer de-identified data.

Website visits

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools. Like any website, our hosting provider records standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, time) for security and reliability. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the services in your engagement letter: bookkeeping, reconciliations, review lists, monthly reports, analyses and recommendations.
  • To communicate with you about your account, questions on your books, invoices and service changes.
  • To meet legal, tax, audit and professional obligations, and to protect against fraud or misuse.

We do not sell or rent personal or business information. We do not use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, combine it with other clients' data to market to anyone, or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Who we share information with

  • People you direct us to, such as your CPA, tax preparer, lender or attorney.
  • Service providers that help us run the business: secure file storage and client portal, email, electronic signature, payment processing and website hosting. They may use the information only to provide their service to us and must protect it. For protected health information, we use only providers that sign a BAA.
  • Intuit, which operates QuickBooks Online and the sign-in and connection process. Intuit's own privacy statement governs the data Intuit holds.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • Business transfer: if JT is merged or sold, client records would transfer only under the same confidentiality commitments, and clients would be told first.

5. Your QuickBooks connection

  • You control it. Only a QuickBooks user with authority over your company can approve the connection, and you can remove it at any time. In QuickBooks Online, go to Apps, then My apps (or Manage apps), find JT Books and choose Disconnect. You can also ask us to disconnect for you.
  • Connection keys. Intuit issues JT Books connection tokens, not your password. We store them only on JT's encrypted work computer, separately for each client, and never email or share them. The tool's own Intuit keys are kept in a protected settings file, not in the program code.
  • When the connection ends (you disconnect, or our engagement ends), we stop all data pulls and delete that client's stored tokens within 30 days, usually the same day.

6. How we protect information

We keep a written information security program built on the FTC Safeguards Rule and, for health information, the HIPAA Security Rule. It includes:

  • Multi-factor authentication on our email, storage, accounting and Intuit accounts.
  • Full-disk encryption on work devices, automatic screen lock and current security updates.
  • Encrypted transfer: files are exchanged through a secure client portal, not ordinary email attachments.
  • Least access: client data is used only by JT staff working on that client. We use read-only access wherever available.
  • Backups, an annual risk assessment and a written incident response plan.

If a security incident affects your information, we will notify you promptly and as required by law (including Georgia's data breach law, the FTC Safeguards Rule and, for health information, HIPAA and the BAA).

7. How long we keep information

  • Client work files and reports: for the length of the engagement, then for up to 2 years so we can answer questions from you or your CPA, unless the law requires longer or you ask us to return or delete them sooner. After that, they are securely deleted.
  • QuickBooks connection tokens: deleted within 30 days of disconnection or the end of the engagement.
  • Protected health information: returned or destroyed within 30 days of the end of the engagement, as the BAA requires.
  • Contact inquiries that do not become clients: deleted within 12 months.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you or your business, correct it, return it, or delete it, and you can disconnect QuickBooks at any time. Email us at the address below. We answer within 30 days. We may keep records we are legally required to keep, and we will tell you if that applies. Because we do not sell or share personal information for advertising, there is nothing to opt out of. We do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track visitors.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date. For material changes, we will tell clients by email before the change takes effect.
