Connect QuickBooks to JT Books

JT Books is the tool JT Reliable Solutions uses to prepare your month-end review and report. It reads your QuickBooks Online data with read-only permission.

How connecting works

  1. During onboarding, we start the connection together, usually on a short screen-share.
  2. Intuit's own QuickBooks sign-in page opens. You sign in (we never see your password), choose your company and click Connect.
  3. That's it. Each month JT Books reads your transactions, accounts, open invoices, receipt attachments and profit and loss report to build your review list and report.

What JT Books can and cannot do

CanCannot
Read accounting data needed for the monthly close Create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks; move money; see your password; read payroll

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time: in QuickBooks Online go to Apps, then My apps (or Manage apps), and choose Disconnect next to JT Books. You can also email us and we will do it. Details: Privacy Policy · End-User License Agreement.
