Power BI and data quality

Every review can be delivered as a Power BI dashboard you own. A data quality audit finds missing, duplicate and impossible values before anyone makes a decision on them.

How it works

  1. Scope call (30 minutes). We agree the question, the export and the success measure. A Business Associate Agreement and engagement letter are signed before any patient data is shared.
  2. Analysis (about 10 business days). Data quality check first, then the analysis. Most reviews need no names or clinical notes.
  3. Findings meeting. The numbers, the worklists, and a 90-day plan with an owner and a measure for each step. JT analyzes and prepares; your staff decide and act.
  4. Optional monthly tracking in a web dashboard or Power BI, or take the files and run it yourself.

Analysis runs on JT's encrypted computer; patient data is never uploaded to outside services. Quality measures are claims-based versions of HEDIS and CMS Star logic for finding gaps, not certified HEDIS results. Risk-score work supports documentation review: only a clinician at a visit can confirm a condition.
