Clean books and clear numbers for businesses, practices and supply chains

JT Reliable Solutions keeps small-business books current every month, and turns clinic, hospital and supply chain data into a short list of fixes worth real dollars.

Monthly bookkeeping

Categorized, reconciled books by the 10th of each month, a short list of questions for you, and a two-page report you can read in five minutes. Works with QuickBooks Online and alongside your CPA.

Healthcare analytics

Denials and underpayments, no-shows, length of stay and readmissions, benchmarked against public CMS data and priced in dollars. Patient data is handled only under a Business Associate Agreement.

Logistics analytics

Inventory, demand, transportation and maintenance data turned into forecasts, service-level and cost dashboards, and a ranked list of savings. See the industries we serve.

Profit and cash consulting

Pricing checks, budgets, 13-week cash plans and monthly KPI dashboards for owner-run businesses.

Logistics and supply chain analytics

Advanced data analytics for the flow of goods: what to stock, where to put it, how to move it and when to service it. We work from the exports your ERP, WMS, TMS or maintenance system already produces, and deliver a working dashboard, the analysis behind it, and a 90-day plan with an owner and a measure for each step.

Healthcare supply chain

Par levels and stockouts for supplies and pharmacy, expiration waste, preference-card and implant spend, and on-time delivery from distributors.

Retail and distribution

Demand forecasting, inventory turns and safety stock, slow and excess stock, fill rate and OTIF, warehouse throughput, and freight cost per order.

Manufacturing and MRO

Spare-parts stocking and criticality, maintenance backlog and downtime, supplier lead-time and quality, and MRO spend consolidation.

Public sector and defense

Fleet and equipment readiness, sustainment and parts demand, and inventory accountability for agencies and contractors, built from publicly available data (contract awards, budgets and published readiness and logistics reports).

Methods include time-series and machine-learning forecasting, inventory optimization, network and route analysis, and predictive maintenance models, with results explained in plain language and built in tools your team already uses (Excel, Power BI or Python).

How the QuickBooks connection works

For bookkeeping clients, our JT Books software connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's secure sign-in with read-only access. We can see your books to prepare reviews and reports. We cannot change records or move money, and you can disconnect at any time. Read more about connecting.
